Wedding haircuts: the 5 we like most

3 November 2026

On the wedding day the haircut is in every photo. In the chair at El Carmen and La Petxina, these are the five wedding haircuts we like most: clean, with character, and easy to style on the day. They work for the groom, the best man and the guest who wants to look finished.

1. Classic side part with a soft taper

A defined part and a low taper. The outline stays sharp and the suit stays the focus. It is the cut that ages best in the album. It works on straight hair or a light wave, with a short, lined-up beard.

2. Slick back

Low volume, a clean neckline and a pomade finish. Formal for the ceremony and still neat at dinner. We recommend it when you want to look dressed up without changing your style overnight.

3. Textured mid fade

Shorter sides and movement on top. This is the one we cut most for daytime weddings when the suit is not stiff. It reads modern in photos and does not demand impossible upkeep the week after.

4. French crop

A short fringe forward and a precise outline. It styles in a minute and holds up in Valencia heat. We like it if you do not want to spend the wedding morning in front of the mirror.

5. Undercut with controlled volume

For hair that is already longer. We take the sides in and leave just enough length on top to comb to the side or back. In the photo it reads as style, not an experiment.

When to cut before the wedding

If the change is big, book 10 to 14 days ahead so you can get used to it. If it is only a tidy-up, two or three days before is the sweet spot: the cut has settled and the lines are still clean. Same-day visits are best kept to a neckline or beard touch-up.

The beard finishes the look

A line-up or a hot-towel shave closes the cut. If you wear a beard, even the length a week before and leave the fine detail for the appointment before the wedding. The menu is on services.

Book at El Carmen or La Petxina and tell us it is for a wedding. We shape the cut to your face, the suit and how you actually style your hair.
