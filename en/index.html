About the barbershop

Miguel’s story

Miguel is the owner of Barber Shop Valencia. A Bolivian entrepreneur, he came to Spain and worked in construction for a while. Seven years ago he started in the barbering trade.

Today he runs two shops in Valencia: La Petxina at Juan Llorens 18, and El Carmen on Calle Baja. The same standard in both chairs: advice, a clean finish and a haircut you notice.

His dream is to open more barbershops across Spain and grow the Barber Shop Valencia brand nationwide.
